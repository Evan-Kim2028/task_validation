# ADR-0015: Pause VEV expansion; validity is artifact counterfactual consistency

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Agree with the command to pause the nine-invariant list. The next layer is three assays: change causality, contract provenance, verifier discrimination. Primary metric remains accepted-tail residual invalidity.

Static first constructions of B and C **failed** as rankers of the 2024 conservative label (AUROC 0.41 and 0.42). Per the same command: drop those constructions. Do not retune. Executable fail-before/pass-after is the only remaining version of assay 1 worth one small trial.

## Why

Material invalidity is disagreement among prompt, gold change, and verifier. Lexical gap scores and file-name witnesses are not that measurement.
