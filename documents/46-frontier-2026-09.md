# The September 2026 frontier: generation, quality control, transfer

The June to September 2026 landscape for generated agentic tasks, their quality control, and the statistics that could have replaced in-population labels. The claim: the frontier generates at scale and filters with models, but nobody bounds residual invalidity, and no 2026 transfer method survives our shift. External numbers are eval-only; none enters a bound (doc 43). Ours cite `data/gold/` paths or doc numbers. arXiv ids cite external work. nr = not reported.

## Part 3. Transfer statistics, and why each fails here

Could old-population labels bound the bad-item rate of a new pool? Six 2026 methods come closest; each needs an assumption doc 40 falsifies here (`data/gold/footprint.holdout.json`).

| Method | What it would give | Assumption it needs | Why doc 40 kills it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Generalized PPI, 2602.10332 | FPR, TPR, AUC of a validity classifier on a new pool | Y given X invariant under covariate shift; overlap | invalidity prevalence shifts: 0.683 swe, 0.078 verified, 0.331 pro, 0.180 tb21, 0 harbor; spec AUROC only 0.48-0.69 across pools |
| MPPI, 2606.21232 | tighter target CIs via multi-source rectifiers | target gold labels plus a density ratio or transport map | no labels on the target pool is the premise; Rogan-Gladen transfer undercovers or goes vacuous in 8 of 10 cells (F1 FAIL) |
| JECS, 2605.21543 | contaminated fraction of a selected subset at most alpha | member-calibration on the same X-law as candidates | member calibration is the human sample we avoid; bounds contamination, not task invalidity |
| Noisy but Valid, 2601.20913 | Type-I control via judge TPR and FPR | judge error rates invariant across populations | gate sensitivity is 0.09 on tb21 vs 0.33 on swe_verified; judge reliability does not transport |
| GRePPI, 2605.29249 | tighter CIs via cross-task recalibration | every task still needs its own labels; sharing is power-only | target pools ship no labels; prevalences span 0 to 0.68, so no shared proxy carries a bound |
| Transport from summaries, 2603.02474 | a target functional from source microdata plus target X-moments | Y given X transportable; summaries capture the shift | we hold full target X (footprint, 3,117 rows) and transfer still fails; RG UCB misses by up to 0.64 |

One fact explains all six: validity is defined per protocol and population, so neither the label law nor judge error rates transport. The frozen F1 predicate failed at 2 of 5 populations (doc 40). The certificate is a probability sample on the shipped pool, never an inference from other benchmarks.

## Part 4. Positioning

Two rules for generated tasks shipped as training data. Decontamination: every shipped task is decontaminated against Terminal-Bench 4.0, Harbor-Index 1.0, Terminal-Bench 2.1, and SWE-bench Verified by n-gram and Jaccard; those populations anchor the footprint, but no generated task may sit near one. Difficulty: admission is a solve-rate band for the target model, FrogNano style, not frontier-unsolved; a third of the hardest Harbor-Index candidates reaching humans were broken (doc 28, doc 31).

| Claim | We claim | We do not claim | Nearest competitor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Residual bound | design-based one-sided 95% UCB on residual invalidity of a frozen accepted pool (docs 30, 35, 41) | that the bound exists elsewhere | OpenAI ~30% Pro broken, ABA 25.7% major: audits without CIs (doc 03) |
| Machine assay | grade-A execution gate certifies execution-verifier tasks (docs 28, 35) | coverage of judge-verifier or no-reference tasks (doc 44) | TB 3.0 oracle, nop, cheat trials; Harbor audit-and-fix |
| Model role | models order the review queue only, never the bound (doc 43) | that a model admission filter certifies anything | T1, CalibForge, Harbor-Index stage 2: model filters, no bound |
| Footprint | non-LLM footprint separates curated from generated at 0.71-0.75 held-out AUROC (doc 40) | that it certifies a new pool or measures taste | SWE-rebench quality heads, CLI-Universe rubric (doc 31) |
| Decontamination | n-gram plus Jaccard against the four populations above before any task ships | that decontamination measures validity | CalibForge 14-gram plus Jaccard (2608.06352) |
| Training difficulty | solve-rate band on the target model, band (0, 0.5] as precedent | frontier-unsolved as a keep rule | Harbor-Index 33% cap; FrogNano band admission (2609.07925) |
| Label transport | no old-population labels bound a new pool (doc 40) | that external audit labels enter any bound; eval-only (doc 43) | the six methods of Part 3, all assumption-blocked |
